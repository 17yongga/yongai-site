## Scope and evidence rules

This brief records first-party homepage and product-page observations. Customer outcomes, client counts, organization sizes, and founder experience below are claims published by the referenced companies; they were not independently verified. The recommended YongAI page does not reuse their language, visual identity, customer proof, or commercial claims.

## Reference 1 — North Group

### Observed positioning

- Page title: `Your AI department, on demand`.
- Hero uses the aggressive headline `Dominate the AI era.` followed by `We plan, build, and deploy your company's AI strategy.`
- The offer spans four categories: Strategy, Training, Transformation, and Fractional AI department.
- The service journey is: free audit → learn how the team works → engineer together → continue month to month.
- Primary conversion is repeated consistently: `Book a free audit`.
- Proof appears through a customer-logo strip and four testimonials.
- Visual system uses a dark red mountain hero, large editorial serif type, warm off-white sections, oversized spacing, and a staged ascent metaphor.

### Transferable principles

- Make continuity explicit: the partner does not stop at advice.
- Show a buyer-friendly progression from first conversation to implementation and ongoing support.
- Use one consistent primary CTA.
- Separate the breadth of the capability model from the practical delivery journey.

### Do not copy

- The mountain/ascent metaphor and red visual identity.
- `Dominate`, `win`, or similarly inflated language; it does not fit YongAI's practical operator voice.
- Logo walls or testimonials without equivalent attributable YongAI client evidence.
- A free-audit promise before the scope and economics are defined.

## Reference 2 — The General Consulting Company

### Observed positioning

- Hero label: `AI Training for Executive Teams`.
- Hero promise: `Get Your Leadership Team AI-Ready`.
- Supporting copy makes the delivery distinction clear: understand AI, evaluate vendors, build an operating strategy, then stay to implement it.
- The problem is framed around vendor overload, board pressure, and the risk of moving too fast or too slowly.
- A detailed case study appears early and is structured as context, scope, outcome, named testimony, and engagement metrics.
- Four programs make commercial depth visible: Coaching, Essentials, Operator, and Deep Build, with published prices/ranges.
- Founder credibility is explicit and personal rather than hidden behind a generic agency voice.
- Solarium acts as a signature diagnostic product: employee interviews → operational map → prioritized AI opportunities → implementation roadmap.
- The homepage continues with process, blog/editorial authority, lead magnet, and repeated call booking.
- Visual system uses a clean white and green institutional palette, sans-serif typography, sparse line-based diagrams, and an ASCII-style founder portrait.

### Transferable principles

- Speak to a specific organizational buyer and a specific pressure.
- Make ways to engage concrete rather than saying only `consulting`.
- Put credible founder/operator experience on the page.
- Use an operational diagnostic or map as the bridge between strategy and implementation.
- Show deliverables and decision outcomes, not only capability names.
- Keep the founder voice factual and based on work actually performed.

### Do not copy

- Case-study outcomes, client details, customer quotes, organization-size proof, and executive counts.
- The four program names, price points, Solarium name, interview model, or ASCII visual device.
- The green visual system.
- Executive-training-first positioning; Gary's strongest differentiated evidence is implementation and operations, not a training-only offer.

## Portfolio architecture recommendation

### YongAI

Parent operator portfolio. Owns the overall point of view, products, and service ventures.

### UrHermes

Audience: individuals and solo founders.  
Delivery unit: one person's private AI operator.  
Core concerns: personal tools, memory, recurring work, approval rules, privacy, and handoff.

### YongAI Systems (working name)

Audience: operating teams and established businesses.  
Delivery unit: a shared business workflow.  
Core concerns: leadership alignment, process mapping, governance, integration, team adoption, and ongoing ownership.

This boundary keeps UrHermes productized and personal while giving the B2B venture room to handle shared data, controls, process ownership, change management, and enterprise implementation.

## Recommended conversion system

- **Primary conversion:** book a fit call around one real workflow.
- **Secondary conversion:** inspect how YongAI Systems works.
- **Tertiary route:** compare Systems with UrHermes.
- Do not launch with a newsletter, generic lead magnet, case-study library, or package pricing until those assets are real.

## Recommended narrative

1. **Grounded hero:** turn one stuck workflow into a working AI system.
2. **Operating gap:** AI pressure arrives before teams understand the real process.
3. **Service model:** choose the workflow → design controls → build/integrate → adopt/improve.
4. **Engagement ladder:** leadership alignment → workflow audit → pilot build → embedded partner.
5. **Recognizable first wins:** service operations, reconciliation/exceptions, management reporting, and knowledge-heavy work.
6. **Portfolio boundary:** UrHermes for one person; Systems for shared operations.
7. **Operator evidence:** Gary's implementation, cutover, reconciliation, platform, and product-building background.
8. **Final CTA:** bring the current process, then decide whether the right next step is alignment, audit, pilot, or no build.

- His background includes platform migration, cutover, operational reconciliation, and wealth operations transformation.

Do not claim yet:

- Measured customer ROI, time savings, adoption, or transformation outcomes.
- A fractional department already serving customers.
- Fixed package pricing, free audits, or delivery timelines.
- Proprietary diagnostic software comparable to Solarium.
- General enterprise AI expertise beyond Gary's supportable implementation and operator evidence.

## Creative direction — Operating Blueprint

Use process maps, numbered operating sequences, handoff gates, decision outcomes, and restrained document-like layouts. Preserve YongAI's black, white, charcoal, and warm-ivory identity. Avoid mountains, green consulting visuals, chat bubbles, circuit patterns, bots, glowing nodes, fake dashboards, and terminal/ASCII imitation.
